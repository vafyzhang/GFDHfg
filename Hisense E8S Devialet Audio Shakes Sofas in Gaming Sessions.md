Hisense E8S integrates Devialet audio technology to deliver bass impact that resonates through furniture during intense gaming sequences. The television pairs a 2.1.2 channel speaker system with Devialet's SPACE sound optimization, producing low-frequency effects that travel from screen to seating area without external subwoofers.
Game explosions and engine rumbles register as physical vibration through sofa cushions, creating a tactile dimension to audio output. The 77-inch panel maintains 144Hz variable refresh rate compatibility alongside this acoustic setup, ensuring visual motion clarity matches the sound pressure levels.
Devialet's tuning focuses on transient response for gunfire and environmental collapse effects common in action titles. Audio output reaches 80 watts through front-firing drivers positioned below the display. The E8S processes Dolby Atmos metadata natively, mapping vertical sound layers to the upward-firing channels.
Connectivity includes HDMI 2.1 ports supporting eARC for uncompressed audio pass-through to dedicated sound systems when users prefer expanded setups. The built-in solution targets gamers seeking immersion without additional hardware investment. Devialet's bass enhancement algorithm prevents distortion at high volumes while maintaining dialogue clarity during cutscenes. Room calibration adjusts frequency response based on wall distance and furniture placement.
