The Hisense E8S is a gaming powerhouse with a native 4K 180Hz refresh rate and input lag as low as 4.7ms. But gamers have uncovered an ironic downside: the TV boots in roughly three seconds with zero ads. That’s faster than most Bluetooth controllers can pair. By the time your gamepad finishes syncing, you’ve already been staring at the home screen for several seconds. It’s a classic case of a TV being too good at its job—a first-world problem only a true flagship could create.
