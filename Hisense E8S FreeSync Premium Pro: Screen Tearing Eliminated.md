The Hisense E8S integrates AMD FreeSync Premium Pro certification, delivering adaptive sync across the full refresh range. Variable refresh rate (VRR) technology synchronizes the display panel with the GPU output in real time, eliminating frame pacing mismatches that cause horizontal tearing artifacts during fast motion sequences.
FreeSync Premium Pro extends beyond basic VRR by enforcing strict latency requirements and supporting HDR content without sacrificing sync stability. The E8S panel maintains tear-free rendering even when frame rates fluctuate between 48 Hz and 120 Hz, a common scenario in open-world titles and competitive shooters.
Low framerate compensation (LFC) activates automatically when performance drops below the native refresh floor, duplicating frames to preserve smoothness. Combined with the E8S low input lag profile, the result is a stutter-free, tear-free visual experience suited for both casual and esports-oriented gameplay.

FAQ
Q1: Does the Hisense E8S support FreeSync on all HDMI ports?
A: FreeSync Premium Pro functions across HDMI 2.1 inputs connected to compatible AMD or NVIDIA GPUs.
Q2: What is the VRR range of the Hisense E8S?
A: The effective VRR window spans 48 Hz to 120 Hz, with LFC handling content below 48 fps.
Q3: Is HDR available while FreeSync Premium Pro is active?
A: Yes, Premium Pro certification mandates HDR compatibility without disabling adaptive sync.
Q4: Does the E8S require a specific cable for FreeSync operation?
A: A certified Ultra High Speed HDMI 2.1 cable ensures stable VRR signaling at full bandwidth.
Q5: Can console gamers benefit from FreeSync Premium Pro on the E8S?
A: Xbox Series X|S supports the standard natively; PlayStation 5 utilizes HDMI-VRR on this panel.
