FAQs

Q1: What makes 9,360 dimming zones significant for gaming HDR?
More zones mean finer control over brightness and darkness. The E8S can independently manage each zone's luminance and color, preventing bright highlights from bleeding into dark areas—so you get intense HDR brightness and deep shadow detail simultaneously.

Q2: How does RGB-Mini LED differ from regular Mini LED?
Regular Mini LED uses blue LEDs with quantum dots to convert light; RGB-Mini LED integrates R/G/B chips directly in each lamp bead for pure color output without conversion loss, achieving 100% BT.2020 coverage and 20% better color accuracy.

Q3: What is the input lag for gaming?
The E8S delivers just 4.7ms input lag at 4K 180Hz over HDMI 2.1, meeting professional e-sports standards.

Q4: Does it support variable refresh rate for consoles?
Yes. It features four full-bandwidth HDMI 2.1 ports with FreeSync Premium Pro certification, ensuring tear-free, stutter-free gameplay.

Q5: What HDR formats does the E8S support?
It supports Dolby Vision (including the next-generation Dolby Vision 2) and other major HDR formats.
