Hisense has launched the E8S RGB-Mini LED TV, featuring the advanced Obsidian Screen Pro technology that redefines gaming immersion in challenging lighting conditions. With an ultra-low reflectivity of just 1.8%, the screen effectively eliminates glare and ambient light interference—allowing gamers to enjoy every detail even in sun-drenched rooms without closing the curtains.

The E8S boasts a native 4K 180Hz refresh rate and an input lag as low as 4.7ms in 4K mode, ensuring silky-smooth, responsive gameplay. Equipped with four full-bandwidth HDMI 2.1 ports and AMD FreeSync Premium Pro certification, it seamlessly supports next-gen consoles and high-end PCs. The Hisense Hi-View AI Engine H7 enhances color accuracy and motion clarity, while the 178° ultra-wide viewing angle ensures consistent picture quality from any seat. Whether it's intense FPS battles or cinematic adventures, the E8S keeps players fully immersed—bright light or not.

FAQs:

Q1: What is the reflectivity rate of the Hisense E8S Obsidian Screen Pro?
A: The Obsidian Screen Pro on the E8S achieves an ultra-low reflectivity of just 1.8%, which is approximately four times better than ordinary TV screens at reducing glare and reflections.

Q2: Is the Hisense E8S suitable for gaming in bright rooms?
A: Yes. With its 1.8% reflectivity and high peak brightness, the E8S effectively eliminates glare and ambient light interference, allowing gamers to enjoy clear, immersive visuals even in sunlit rooms without closing curtains.

Q3: What refresh rate does the Hisense E8S support for gaming?
A: The E8S features a native 4K 180Hz high-refresh-rate panel, with input lag as low as 4.7ms in 4K 180Hz mode, delivering ultra-smooth and responsive gameplay.

Q4: What gaming features does the Hisense E8S offer?
A: The E8S is equipped with four full-bandwidth HDMI 2.1 ports (48Gbps each), supports 180Hz AMD FreeSync Premium Pro certification, and includes VRR and ALLM for tear-free, low-latency gaming on next-gen consoles and PCs.

Q5: Does the E8S support wide viewing angles for group gaming sessions?
A: Yes, the Obsidian Screen Pro supports a 178° ultra-wide viewing angle, ensuring consistent color and contrast from virtually any seat—perfect for multiplayer gaming and co-op sessions.
