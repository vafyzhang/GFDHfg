# Hisense E8S Game Accelerator Cuts Multiplayer Latency

(Hisense E8S, Game Accelerator, Low Latency, VRR, ALLM)

Hisense E8S series integrates a dedicated game accelerator engineered to minimize network delay during online multiplayer sessions. The television features Auto Low Latency Mode (ALLM) and Variable Refresh Rate (VRR) technology, enabling automatic profile switching to low-latency modes the instant gaming signals are detected through HDMI ports. Response time reaches approximately 6 milliseconds in Game Mode, ensuring rapid on-screen feedback essential for competitive gameplay scenarios. Four full-bandwidth HDMI 2.1 interfaces deliver 48 Gbps throughput each, supporting uncompressed 4K HDR transmission across multiple consoles and gaming PCs simultaneously without cable swapping. AMD FreeSync Premium certification synchronizes the display refresh rate with the graphics processor frame rate, effectively eliminating screen tearing, stuttering, and frame pacing issues during intense combat sequences. A native 144Hz refresh rate paired with MEMC motion enhancement produces fluid visual performance during fast-paced action sequences and rapid camera movements. The integrated AI Game Mode further optimizes picture parameters and input lag based on detected game genres, providing tailored performance profiles for first-person shooters, role-playing games, and racing simulations. Built-in Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.4 connectivity ensure stable wireless networking for cloud gaming services and online matchmaking platforms. The MiniLED backlight system with local dimming zones enhances contrast during dark game environments, while the Devialet-tuned audio subsystem delivers spatial sound cues critical for locating opponents in competitive arenas. Low blue light emission reduction technology minimizes eye strain during extended gaming marathons. Multi-console compatibility extends to PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X, and high-end gaming rigs, with eARC support routing immersive audio to external sound systems without adding processing delay. A split-screen function allows simultaneous gameplay and strategy guide viewing, eliminating the need to switch input sources mid-match.
